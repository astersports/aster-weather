What v0.8.0 is
  Nine public functions: getWeather (every part in one report), getNow, getHourly, getDaily,
  getAlerts, getRadarRain, getSun, getMoon, getEventWeather. Plus clearWeatherCaches, the
  condition helpers, their types, and the age and retry limits as constants.
  Station readings, forecasts and the event reading carry a Stamp: kind (observed or forecast),
  the time, the fetch time, stale, source. Past its maximum age a reading is dropped.
  Radar may only add rain. A frame older than 20 minutes never claims.
  US coverage only. Outside it the answer is null. The engine never throws.
  The five Open-Meteo functions are deprecated and keep their behaviour. They are removed in
  v1.0.0, after the consumers move.
  README.md is the reference for all of it. Every number there is read from a constant in code.
